Still Have Questions?

Do you require a deposit before starting work?

No. At The Drain Man Inc., we do not believe in deposits. An honest and hard-working company shouldn't need your money before the job starts. You pay only when we have delivered the work we promised.

Are you licensed and insured in the City of Toronto?

Yes. We are Master Licensed Plumbing Contractors. Every member of our team is fully insured and WSIB certified, ensuring that both our family and yours are protected throughout the project.

How do you find the exact location of a drain blockage?

We use high-definition Video Camera Inspection technology. This allows us to see inside your pipes in real-time and pinpoint the exact depth and location of the issue, which saves you thousands in unnecessary excavation costs.

What areas of the GTA do you serve?

Our office is located in Scarborough, but we serve the entire Greater Toronto Area, including North York, Etobicoke, East York, and Downtown Toronto.
